# Classroom Procedures for Substitutes

Take attendance in the first five minutes using the seating chart on the podium.
Chromebooks are only used when the plan calls for them and stay closed during instructions.
Collect all student work at the end of class and leave it in the labeled tray on the teacher's desk.
Substitutes may not grade student work.
Early finishers read independently; there is no free time on devices.
Students may use the hall pass one at a time; sign out on the clipboard.
