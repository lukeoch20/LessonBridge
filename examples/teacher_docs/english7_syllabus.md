# English 7 — Course Syllabus (2026–2027)

Welcome to seventh-grade English. We read widely, write daily, and talk about ideas with evidence.

## Materials
Students need a composition notebook, a binder, and an independent reading book every day. Chromebooks are provided in class.

## Course Outline

Quarter 1
Unit 1: Launching Readers and Writers (6 days)
Unit 2: Short Fiction — Plot, Conflict and Character (18 days)
Unit 3: Narrative Writing (12 days)

Quarter 2
Unit 4: Argumentative Writing — claims, evidence, counterclaims (16 days)
Unit 5: Nonfiction — Structure, Purpose and Main Idea (15 days)

Quarter 3
Unit 6: Novel Study — Theme and Character Development (22 days)
Unit 7: Research and Source Evaluation (12 days)

Quarter 4
Unit 8: Poetry and Media Literacy (12 days)
Unit 9: Reading SOL Review and Reflective Writing (14 days)

## Assessments
Unit tests and essays are weighted 50%; quizzes 25%; classwork and reading responses 25%.
On quiz days, students receive 15 minutes to study before the quiz.

## Classroom Expectations
Late work is accepted for up to one week at a 10% deduction.
Early finishers read independently; there is no free time on devices.
Substitutes may not grade student work; all work is collected and left for the teacher.
Homework is due at the start of class in the homework tray.
