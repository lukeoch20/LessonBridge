# Civics and Economics — Course Syllabus (2026–2027)

This course prepares students for the Civics & Economics SOL and for life as informed citizens.

## Course Outline

Quarter 1
Unit 1: Civics Skills and Foundations of Government (10 days)
Unit 2: Founding Documents (14 days)
Unit 3: Citizenship and Civic Participation (10 days)

Quarter 2
Unit 4: The Political Process (12 days)
Unit 5: The National Government (16 days)
Unit 6: Federalism and Virginia's Government (12 days)

Quarter 3
Unit 7: Local Government and Public Policy (12 days)
Unit 8: The Judicial Systems (12 days)

Quarter 4
Unit 9: Economics (20 days)
Unit 10: Civics & Economics SOL Review (12 days)

## Assessments
Unit tests are weighted 50%; quizzes 25%; classwork and projects 25%.
On quiz days, students receive 15 minutes to study before the quiz.

## Classroom Expectations
Students may use the hall pass one at a time; sign out on the clipboard.
Current events articles are due every other Friday.
